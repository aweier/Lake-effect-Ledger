# Lake Effect Ledger character bible

Developer-facing reference. **This file contains story spoilers, private
motivations, and author-only continuity notes. Never render it during gameplay.**

The `Player` record in `content/characters.yaml` is a runtime placeholder, not a
recurring authored character, so it does not receive a bible entry. Its public
role is **Junior Commodity Risk Analyst**; name and education remain
player-selected.

The NEW CONTACT card may render only `name`, `role`, `origin`,
`public_detail`, and `interaction_reason` from `content/characters.yaml`.
Legacy-ID notes, family links, secrets, motivations, and reveal plans are
author-only.

## Evelyn Marsh

- Stable ID: `evelyn_marsh`
- Canonical display identity: Evelyn Marsh
- Role and authority boundaries: Controller. Owns close governance, accounting
  review, classification, reconciliations, and management-response drafting.
  She may approve documented corrective action within her authority; she does
  not transmit trades, set commercial forecasts, or interpret the revolver for
  treasury.
- Birthplace and places lived: Milwaukee; Sheboygan early in her career;
  Milwaukee again.
- Professional route: UW–Milwaukee accounting; manufacturing cost accounting;
  energy accounting; Northstar controllership.
- Family background: One parent worked in municipal payroll and the other
  maintained machine tools. She now helps an aging aunt remain independent.
- Non-work anchors: Repairs mechanical clocks; volunteers at a neighborhood tax
  clinic during filing season.
- Midwest decision: Returned to Milwaukee for family and stayed because the
  neighborhood obligations feel chosen rather than inherited.
- Public personality: Exact, dry, economical with praise.
- Private motivation: Keep Northstar governable when its owners prefer personal
  understandings to explicit authority.
- Personal aspiration: Restore her grandfather's regulator clock and take a
  train trip without carrying a close calendar.
- Personal disappointment: A promising junior accountant once left after
  Evelyn mistook endurance for mentorship.
- Strength: Separates records, judgments, and outcomes.
- Weakness: Can mistake emotional distance for neutrality.
- Contradiction: Protects junior staff through rigid process while making that
  protection feel like another test.
- Relationship to Northstar: Institutional memory and accounting boundary.
- Relationship to the player: Supervisor, gatekeeper, and possible sponsor.
- Other major relationships: Respects Marisol's source facts; constrains Cal's
  authority; challenges Vince; understands Dom's power without treating it as
  accounting evidence; relies on June for cash timing and Noah for independent
  testing.
- Voice guidance: Short declarative sentences, concrete record nouns, dry
  analogies, and questions whose real subject is accountability.
- Topics understood: Close, classification, evidence, approval, reconciliation,
  management response, and accounting implications of liquidity.
- Outside her authority: Trade transmission, physical nominations, commercial
  forecasts, FCM requirements, and final treasury funding decisions.
- Story secrets: She has documented several management overrides without yet
  deciding whether Northstar can correct itself internally.
- Reveal beats:
  - Public introduction — First Rotation Day 1 NEW CONTACT card. Ordinary public
    information; establishes the player's supervisor and accounting boundary.
  - First personal reveal — `evelyn_approval_request` in Hedge Book. Ordinary
    office conversation; the repaired clock makes her precision feel practiced,
    not mechanical.
  - Deeper conditional reveal — `ec_risk_emphasis` after
    `write_accurate_hedge_memo`. Private but non-confidential conversation about
    her aunt and tax-clinic work; shows that her evidence discipline protects
    people who arrive with incomplete records.
- Cultural-writing cautions: Do not reduce Milwaukee identity to beer or
  politeness. Her restraint is professional, not regional timidity. Do not make
  caregiving proof of virtue.
