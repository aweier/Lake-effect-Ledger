# Lake Effect Ledger character bible

Developer-facing reference. **This file contains story spoilers and author-only
motivations. Never render it during gameplay.**

The public introduction fields in `content/characters.yaml` are the only
biographical fields used by the NEW CONTACT card. Later details belong in short
authored dialogue and must remain conditional where identified below.

## Evelyn Marsh

- Stable ID: `evelyn_marsh`
- Full name: Evelyn Marsh
- Pronunciation: No special guidance.
- Age range: Early to mid-50s.
- Role: Controller.
- Birthplace and places lived: Milwaukee; brief early-career period in
  Sheboygan; returned to Milwaukee.
- Education or professional route: Accounting degree from UW–Milwaukee;
  manufacturing cost accounting, then energy accounting and controllership.
- Family background: Lower-middle-class Milwaukee family; one parent worked in
  municipal payroll and the other maintained machine tools.
- Public personality: Exact, dry, economical with praise.
- Private motivation: Keep Northstar governable even when its owners prefer
  personal understandings to explicit authority.
- Strength: Separates records, judgments, and outcomes.
- Weakness: Can mistake emotional distance for neutrality.
- Contradiction: Protects junior staff through rigid process while making that
  protection feel like another test.
- Relationship to Northstar: Institutional memory and control boundary.
- Relationship to the player: Supervisor, gatekeeper, and possible sponsor.
- Voice guidance: Short declarative sentences; concrete record nouns; questions
  whose real subject is accountability.
- Topics understood: Close, evidence, authorization, reconciliation, liquidity.
- Topics she would not explain: Trading strategy beyond the control implications.
- **Story secrets:** She has documented several management overrides without
  yet deciding whether Northstar can correct itself internally.
- Planned reveal beats: Public card in First Rotation; professional history
  through approval dialogue; conditional concern after formal escalation or
  delayed notice.
- Cultural-writing cautions: Do not reduce Milwaukee identity to beer or
  politeness. Her restraint is professional, not regional timidity.
